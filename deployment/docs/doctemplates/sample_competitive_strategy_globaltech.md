## 5. BATTLE TACTICS

### 5.1 Trap Setting for Competitors

**Accenture Trap: "Scale vs. Agility"**
- **Setup:** Ask about dedicated team and rotation policies
- **Expected Response:** They'll talk about "deep bench" and "resource flexibility"
- **Counter:** "We assign dedicated teams for continuity. Isn't rotation disruptive?"
- **Impact:** Exposes their high turnover and lack of commitment

**Accenture Trap: "Innovation Showcase"**
- **Setup:** Challenge all vendors to demo their AI/ML platform live
- **Expected Response:** They'll show generic cloud services or partner solutions
- **Counter:** We demo our proprietary platform with manufacturing use cases
- **Impact:** Exposes their lack of differentiated IP

**Deloitte Trap: "24x7 Managed Services"**
- **Setup:** Ask how they'll deliver managed services requirement
- **Expected Response:** Admit they'll subcontract or partner
- **Counter:** "We offer end-to-end. No handoffs, single SLA, one throat to choke."
- **Impact:** Exposes their capability gap and integration risk

**Infosys Trap: "Local Presence & Partnership"**
- **Setup:** Ask about Chicago-based team and executive engagement
- **Expected Response:** Talk about global delivery and virtual collaboration
- **Counter:** "Our Chicago delivery center means daily partnership, not email."
- **Impact:** Exposes their offshore model and cultural fit concerns

### 5.2 Competitive Landmines to Avoid

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Directly attack competitors by name (comes across as desperate)
- ❌ Focus only on price (commoditizes our value)
- ❌ Overpromise timeline or outcomes (credibility risk)
- ❌ Dismiss their concerns about our brand vs. Accenture
- ❌ Bad-mouth their previous Accenture experience (unprofessional)

**DO:**
- ✓ Focus on our unique value and differentiators
- ✓ Let facts and data speak (case studies, references, metrics)
- ✓ Acknowledge competitors' strengths gracefully
- ✓ Position based on customer needs, not our features
- ✓ Build trust through transparency and guarantees

### 5.3 Objection Handling Playbook

**Objection: "You're not as big as Accenture"**
- **Probe:** "Help me understand - is size important for scale, or for confidence?"
- **Empathize:** "I understand the comfort of the biggest brand."
- **Respond:** "We're $2.5B with 15,000 employees - Fortune 1000 ourselves. More importantly, we've successfully delivered projects this size and larger. Can we show you references from companies who chose us over Accenture?"
- **Confirm:** "If we can prove comparable scale and superior outcomes, does size matter?"

**Objection: "Your price is higher than Infosys"**
- **Probe:** "Is lowest price the primary decision criterion, or best value?"
- **Empathize:** "Budget discipline is important, especially in today's environment."
- **Respond:** "Our TCO analysis shows we're actually 12% more cost-effective over 3 years when you factor in quality, fewer overruns, and cost optimization results. Would you like to see the detailed comparison?"
- **Confirm:** "If we prove better TCO and lower risk, is initial price flexible?"

**Objection: "We have a relationship with Deloitte"**
- **Probe:** "What aspects of that relationship are most valuable?"
- **Empathize:** "Advisory relationships are valuable and we respect that."
- **Respond:** "The question is whether advisory expertise alone delivers a technical transformation. We bring strategic thinking AND deep implementation capability. Would it make sense to leverage Deloitte for advisory and us for execution?"
- **Confirm:** "If we demonstrate superior technical capability, can we earn your trust?"

**Objection: "This project is too important to take a risk on someone new"**
- **Probe:** "What would make you confident in our ability to deliver?"
- **Empathize:** "This is a career-defining project and we understand the stakes."
- **Respond:** "We've delivered 15+ transformations this size with 99%+ success rate. We're willing to put 10% of fees at risk tied to outcomes. Can we introduce you to 3 CIOs who took the same 'risk' and are now our biggest advocates?"
- **Confirm:** "If our references validate our track record, does that address the risk concern?"
